Help Chatbot for Schools & Colleges

This is the real chatbot running on the 10 websites of SKANS School System. Open it from the round S button at the bottom-left of this page.

1. Choose how the chat should behave

On a campus website the chat knows the campus from the web address and only talks about that campus. Links open the real campus pages.

2. Try typing

  • class 5 fee
  • 5 class ki fee
  • kg fees
  • documents required
  • admision kaise
  • fees kitni hai
  • minimum age
  • hello
  • who are you

Real fee amounts appear for AECHS Campus, read live from that campus website.

What it does

  • Topic cards and common questions, with a link to the right page of the right campus
  • Understands English and Roman Urdu, and small spelling mistakes
  • Real fee amounts per class, from the campus Fee Structure
  • WhatsApp and Call buttons for the campus under every answer
  • No AI service, no API key, no database, no monthly cost

Source code, setup guide and screenshots: github.com/farooq831/Chatbot_for_School-College
